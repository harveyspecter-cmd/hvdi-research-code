## Scale-out: InfiniBand's numbers are adoption, and UET is still a specification

### InfiniBand E stays at 1 because Meta reports parity, not benefit

The only operator document found that puts InfiniBand and Ethernet at the same boundary is Meta's 2024 infrastructure post: **two clusters of 24,576 H100 GPUs each** (OBS; March 2024; one RoCE fabric, one NVIDIA Quantum-2 InfiniBand fabric), built so that Meta could judge both interconnects for large-scale training, both reported as free of network bottlenecks, with Llama 3 trained on the RoCE cluster ([S87](https://engineering.fb.com/2024/03/12/data-center-engineering/building-metas-genai-infrastructure/)). The post gives no job-completion time, cost or power comparison. Under the rubric, level 2 requires one buyer-reported benefit; a statement that Ethernet matched InfiniBand is evidence about Ethernet's economics. The score therefore stays at 1, with the basis changed from "none" to "buyer, qualitative, no benefit reported".

The hard numbers that exist for InfiniBand are adoption and revenue evidence and belong in the A cell. NVIDIA reported **Data Center networking revenue of $15 billion** (OBS; fiscal Q1 FY2027, quarter ended late April 2026; all NVIDIA data-centre networking, not InfiniBand alone) and InfiniBand growth of more than 4x year over year (OBS growth rate; issuer statement; no dollar amount), attributed to XDR deployments ([S85](https://s201.q4cdn.com/141608511/files/doc_financials/2027/q1/NVDA-Q1-2027-Earnings-Call-20-May-2026-5_00-PM-ET.pdf)). The next quarter's call gives networking growth of 18% sequentially (OBS; fiscal Q2 FY2027; no dollar figure) and no InfiniBand figure at all ([S86](https://s201.q4cdn.com/141608511/files/content_files/TRANSCRIPT_-NVIDIA-Corp-NVDA-US-Q2-2027-Earnings-Call-26-August-2026-5_00-PM-ET.pdf)). Dell'Oro estimates that InfiniBand sales more than tripled in 1Q 2026 (EST; AI back-end switch sales) and attributes a significant part of that demand to brownfield upgrades of the installed base, while Ethernet took about two-thirds of AI-cluster switch sales (EST; 1Q 2026; revenue; denominator AI back-end switch sales) ([S11](https://www.delloro.com/newsroom/ethernet-extends-lead-in-ai-scale-out-networks-despite-strong-infiniband-rebound/)). For FY2025 Dell'Oro puts Ethernet at more than double InfiniBand in AI scale-out (EST; FY2025; revenue) and names vendor diversity and supply constraints, not performance economics, as the driver ([S84](https://www.delloro.com/news/ethernet-more-than-doubles-size-of-infiniband-as-the-leading-fabric-for-ai-scale-out-networks-in-2025/)). The 2Q 2026 release gives no share figure and does not mention InfiniBand ([S29](https://www.delloro.com/news/ai-back-end-networks-switch-sales-surpass-front-end-networks-for-the-first-time-in-2q2026/)). Repeat purchase is revealed preference; it is not documented economics. Confirmed gaps: job-completion time, cost and power on a matched workload; InfiniBand revenue in dollars; whether Meta's SIGCOMM 2024 paper (S10) contains any InfiniBand comparison, which was not re-read. A widely circulated blog table asserting that Ethernet costs about one-third of InfiniBand cites no source and is excluded ([S88](https://techblog.comsoc.org/2026/10/13/2026-ocp-summit-full-stack-ai-networking-silicon-from-nvidia-cisco-ethernet-vs-infiniband-interconnects/)).

### UET T, A, Q and E: four cells, one buyer sentence in the future tense

The strongest technical evidence for UET is the consortium's own version list: 1.0 on 11 June 2025, 1.0.1 on 5 September 2025, 1.0.2 on 28 January 2026, 1.0.3 on 16 July 2026 and 1.1 on 12 October 2026 (SPEC) ([S72](https://ultraethernet.org/specification)). **Two of the three maintenance releases corrected congestion-control algorithms** (the RCCC source algorithm in 1.0.1 and CMS in 1.0.2), which is the profile of a specification still being reconciled with implementations. Compliance is reported as self-attestation against checklists; a UEC official is reported as declining to comment on interoperable products ([S75](https://www.networkworld.com/article/4235340/ultra-ethernet-1-1-extends-the-specification-to-scale-up-networks-and-adds-programmable-congestion-control.html)). The UEC compliance page lists documents only: no test lab, no compliant-product list, no plugfest and no interoperability result ([S73](https://ultraethernet.org/compliance/)). It follows that a "UEC-compliant" label is itself a vendor claim under the admission rule. Silicon status is vendor-stated: AMD's Pollara 400 was shipping to interested parties in June 2025 (vendor claim) ([S44](https://www.tomshardware.com/networking/amd-deploys-its-first-ultra-ethernet-ready-network-card-pensando-pollara-provides-up-to-400-gbps-performance)); Broadcom's Thor Ultra was sampling to select customers on 14 October 2025 (vendor claim), which predates the 1.0.2 CMS correction, so any compliance statement should be tied to a specification version ([S79](https://www.storagereview.com/news/broadcom-thor-ultra-uec-compliant-800g-ai-ethernet-nic-for-100k-xpu-scale-out?amp)). The design paper by Hoefler and co-authors (arXiv:2508.08906) was read only through a trade-press summary, is co-authored by member-company staff and is described as a design overview without measurements ([S77](https://semiengineering.com/overview-ultra-ethernets-design-and-architectural-advancements-eth-zurich-broadcom-hpe-et-al/)). One caution for the ledger: the UEC "Member Declarations" register is a patent-declaration list (20 entries, January 2025 to August 2026), not a product-compliance list, and must not be cited as compliance evidence ([S74](https://ultraethernet.org/member-declarations/)). No document found states which transport, RoCEv2 or UET, runs on Oracle's Pollara-equipped networks or on AMD Helios racks. AMD's 3 November 2026 call names five customers with Helios racks online and does not mention Pollara, Pensando, Ultra Ethernet or NICs ([S80](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/amd/transcripts/724028-q3-2026/)); none of the three Dell'Oro back-end releases read mentions Ultra Ethernet, UEC or RoCE (S11, S29, [S84](https://www.delloro.com/news/ethernet-more-than-doubles-size-of-infiniband-as-the-leading-fabric-for-ai-scale-out-networks-in-2025/)). The distinction between UEC-capable hardware deployed and the UET protocol in use is the central measurement problem for this cell. A stays at 2 as "one buyer-stated deployment intention", with confidence lowered to low and a downward sensitivity to 1. The same finding bears on the dataset's UET milestone ("UEC-compliant 800G NICs ship inside AMD Helios racks"): the two AMD calls read do not support the claim that UET is the transport in those racks.

For capital quality the finding is a confirmed absence in the three calls read. ## Conclusion

The research pass changes what the 18 cells rest on more than what they score. Before it, several scores of 3 stood on secondary write-ups or on inference from revenue; after it, two of them stand on buyer-authored talks whose existence is confirmed and whose contents are unread, and one capital-quality score stands on a filing read through a mirror. The index is therefore more defensible in source class and no more certain in level. The weak cells also fall into two different kinds. Some are weak because a document exists and was not opened (the two Meta decks, the AMD exhibits, the Dell'Oro update); a day of manual reading could move Ethernet scale-up T to 3, firm or drop LPO A and merchant DPU T, and raise confidence on three others. The rest are weak because the evidence does not exist in public: fabric-level economics for NVLink, UALink and Ethernet scale-up, UET in measured production use, and product-level revenue for LPO, UET NICs and merchant DPUs. Those are structural gaps, and the index should report them as GAP.

Two implications for the framework follow. First, the category definitions now carry as much weight as the scores: the deployed Ethernet scale-up systems are not the ESUN or SUE systems the row names, UEC 1.1 extends UET into the same territory, and the merchant DPU row leans on evidence about in-house devices. A reviewer will test these boundaries before testing any score, so each row needs a written inclusion rule dated to the evidence cut-off. Second, the admission rule needs a companion rule on reading depth. A buyer-authored talk known only by its title satisfies the source-class test for 3 and says nothing about scale or results. Recording read status beside source class, as the ledger above does, would let the Monte Carlo treat "buyer, contents unread" as a wider uncertainty band than "buyer, read at origin".
